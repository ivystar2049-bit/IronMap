# IronMap privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

IronMap does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- **Your workout data** (sessions, sets, equipment, custom exercises, settings) is stored
  only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never uploaded.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.** The app contains no
  third-party SDKs that collect data.
- **Links you tap.** Some exercises show a link to a tutorial search on YouTube; tapping it
  opens your browser and is subject to that site's policy. If you add an image or video URL
  to a custom exercise, the app loads it from that address.
- **Deleting your data.** Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  Because nothing is sent anywhere, there is nothing held about you to delete elsewhere.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ivystar2049-bit/IronMap/issues
